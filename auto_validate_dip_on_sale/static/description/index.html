One Click Delivery, Invoice, Payment

This module automates key sales processes by immediately validating Delivery Orders, generating Invoices, and processing Payments upon Sale Order confirmation. Additionally, it provides alerts for low-stock products in Sale Orders, applicable only for companies where this feature is enabled.

Key Highlights

Automates Delivery, Invoice, and Payment processing based on Sale Order confirmation.

Supports partial automation:
Delivery and Invoice
Delivery Only

Allows selecting the payment journal directly on the Sale Order.

Provides low-stock alerts for products in Sale Orders.

Feature is company specific and active only where enabled.

Configure Sales Automation

Click on Settings>>Users & Companies >> Companies >>Select the (Company) >>Sales Automation tab
Configure the Automations
1)Check Auto Validate Delivery.
2)Auto Create & Post Invoice
3)Auto Register Payment
Whichever automation is selected, its corresponding functionality will be activated and implemented within the company.

★ Features

Available in Odoo 18.0 Community and Enterprise
Full Automation: Validates Delivery Orders, creates Invoices, and processes Payments upon Sale Order confirmation.
Partial Automation: Validates Delivery Orders and creates Invoices upon Sale Order confirmation.
Delivery-Only Automation: Validates Delivery Orders upon Sale Order confirmation.
Allows selecting the payment journal directly on the Sale Order.
Low-Stock Alerts: Warns about products with insufficient stock in Sale Orders.
Company-Specific: Features are active only for companies where enabled.
